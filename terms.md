<h2 id="en">Terms of Use (English)</h2>

### 1. The service

WhichToKeep (called HangisiKalsın in Turkish) is a mobile app published by **Coditive** that helps you find and remove photos and videos you no longer want, on your own phone. It is provided "as is". How the app treats your data is described in the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy#en' | relative_url }}).

### 2. Free use and Plus

The app is free to download and use. Free use includes the full scan and every category, ready-made filter results, your first clean-up with a ready-made filter, the gallery report, the safety net and 50 free decisions a day. **Plus** adds unlimited decisions, clean-up with every ready-made filter, search in your own words, "find more like this", saved filters and one-tap weekly clean-up in simple mode.

Plus is optional and has two forms:

- **Yearly subscription.** It renews automatically every year through your store account unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel at any time in your Apple Account settings (Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions). Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid period.
- **Lifetime.** A single payment that does not renew and stays valid for the same store account.

There is **no weekly subscription** and **no auto-renewing free trial**. Prices are shown by the store in your currency at the moment of purchase. Payments and refunds are handled by the store you downloaded the app from, under its refund policy. "Restore purchases" in the Plus screen re-checks your store account.

### 3. You decide what is deleted

The app **suggests**. Matches, "reasons" such as sharper or eyes open, and ready-made filter results are automatic estimates and can be wrong. Look before you decide.

Nothing is deleted without your confirmation. A "trash" decision moves an item to the app's own trash, where you can restore it. Deletion only starts when you tap "Empty trash" and confirm in your phone's own dialog. On iPhone deleted photos move to Recently Deleted for about 30 days; if iCloud Photos is on, the deletion applies to your other devices too. On Android a confirmed deletion is permanent and the space is freed at once. Albums you protect are never suggested; on iPhone, favorites are never suggested either. You remain responsible for what you confirm. To the extent the law allows, Coditive is not liable for items you choose to delete.
